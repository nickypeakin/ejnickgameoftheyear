Single-player survival · Island seed based

Rustbound

You wash up on a beach with a rock and a torch. Gather, craft, build walls before dark, and raid the outpost if you dare.

Rustbound needs a keyboard and mouse. Saves stay in this browser.

Your first night

  1. Gather. Rock on trees for wood, on stone for stone. E picks up hemp.
  2. Craft. Stone hatchet and pickaxe, then a building plan and hammer.
  3. Build. Foundations, walls, a doorway and a wooden door.
  4. Settle. Sleeping bag, campfire for meat, a box for your loot.
  5. Push out. Furnace, workbench, then the outpost's military crates.
